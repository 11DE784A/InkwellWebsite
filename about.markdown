# About Inkwell

The SISSA Inkwell is a community publication created by and for the diverse
members of SISSA: students, postdocs, staff, professors, and everyone in
between. With the dedicated support of some amazing individuals here at SISSA,
we aim to produce this magazine as a space to spark discussions that challenge
us to step outside our comfort zones and to help us explore the world beyond
our own perspectives.  

While we have various platforms to discuss scientific topics and research, The
Inkwell provides a unique space for creative expression. Here, you can share
your thoughts, stories, and artistic endeavours, fostering a sense of belonging
and understanding within our diverse community. We invite you to contribute and
help illuminate the rich tapestry of experiences that make up SISSA, allowing
us all to see the beauty in the worlds we carry. 

Want your work to be featured in the next issue? See our [submission
guidelines](/submit).
