_Trieste center_

__Marco__ I am not sure what you’re saying, you know. I thought you wanted this, Giorgin. 

__Giorgio__ Actually, I did but my mind changed. 

__Marco__ Well, how was I supposed to know, you could have sent me a message or something. We’re now stuck. What are we going to do? 

__Giorgio__ Not sure. Maybe take the train to Venice and figure it out. 

__Marco__ But that’s like more than two hours. By the time we get there we lost our chance. 

__Giorgio__ What chance? 

__Marco__ You know, the plan. 

__Giorgio__ What plan? 

__Marco__ Come on, I already told you. 

__Giorgio__ What do you mean? What you told me last week was a bunch of crap. 

_They stand on Piazza Sant’Antonio in silence in the rain_

__Giorgio__ Anyway, we’re not standing here in the rain waiting for nothing. I need that money. 

__Marco__ Exactly, that’s why we have to do this, like, you know, subito. The guy is really into it. 

__Giorgio__ Not that simple, is it? 

_Silence_

__Giorgio__ You’re such a...

__Marco__ A what? 

__Giorgio__ Forget it. 

__Marco__ _[under his breath]_ Che stronzo. 

__Giorgio__ Anyway, let’s go take a coffee. 

_They go in and sit at a table in the Caffè Stella Polare._

__Marco__ Un capo in B, per piacere. 

__Giorgio__ Per me un nero, grazie. 

__Marco__ You know what? We don’t need to go to Venice or whatever. We just invite the mark here and show him around Trieste. 

__Giorgio__ And then what? 

_Marco picks up his cellphone and dials._

__Marco__ _[a bit too loud, waving his hands]_ Good morning, sir. How are you doing? Enjoying the Biennale?  
...  
Good, good. Listen, I have the thing here with me.  
...  
Yeah, yeah. It’s right here. My mate Giorgio and I are admiring it right now. Beautiful design and those colors...  
...  
Giorgio? Oh, he’s my mate, for years. No problem.  
...  
Absolutely, has the signature and everything.  
...  
Any chance you could come to Trieste today? We got another guy who’s very keen but, you know, first come, first served.  
...  
Never been to Trieste? Oh, you should definitely come then, we can show you around. Beautiful city. You know, meaning of nowhere and everything.  
...  
OK, OK, fine, yeah, yeah, call me back. _[fast and clipped]_ Chuh, chuh, chuh, ciao.  

__Giorgio__ What did he say? What? 

_Pause_

Who’s the other guy? 

__Marco__ I think he will come. He needs to check with his wife, the sucker. Carino. 

__Giorgio__ Where should we take them? 

__Marco__ How about we show them around a bit first? Establish a connection, a
good _[making air quotes]_ rapport, tra virgolette, you know what I mean. We
take i vecchietti to via Cavana, Piazza Unità, the canal, la chiesa di
Sant’Antonio, the Serbian Orthodox church. They are really into churches, you
know. By then they are tired, you know, we bring them here, James Joyce and
everything. We sit them in the room at the back, quiet, kind of dark. The old
man and his wife facing the entrance. 

__Giorgio__ And then what? 

__Marco__ Well, we have our thing in a simple shopping bag, unobtrusive, you know what I mean, Aldi say. We have it wrapped in a piece of nice cloth, maybe purple or something. But we first ask the guy to show us the money. In contanti, Giorgino, 500 euro bills. No bullshit. 

__Giorgio__ Anyway, the guy is an art dealer, he will figure out it’s a fake in no time. 

__Marco__ That’s the beauty, you know. It doesn’t matter! We get our coffees and brioscine, maritozzi or whatever, chit-chat about Trieste, you know, what they visited. 

__Giorgio__ Per me alla crema. Who pays the stuff? 

__Marco__ Then I pull the thing out of the bag and while the guy is looking at it you get up to go to the bathroom. Very polite, you know. “I am sorry, signori, if you will excuse me, I need to run by the toilette” or whatever. Then the couple is looking at the thing, turning it around, you know, and before the guy even starts to show any doubts, you come out of the toilette and point the gun to his back. 

__Giorgio__ What gun? 

__Marco__ Bellissimo, Giorgin, you see, that’s the best part, it’s a fake gun! When you come out of the toilette, you know, you have their back. So you approach our table real slow, you know, hiding the gun. Like a cat, you know what I mean. 

_He moves his arms mimicking a cat about to catch a little bird_

And then, boom! You press the gun to the guy’s back, between the shoulders, like this, you know. 

_He shows Giorgio how_

And you go real calm: “Quiet now, or I shoot. Just leave the bag with the money on the table and get the hell out without even a peep.” _[to himself]_ Che bellezza! 

__Giorgio__ How do you know the money is in a bag? 

__Marco__ Ma Giorgino, caro, where else will it be? And, who cares! He has the money, we take it, you know. 

__Giorgio__ Anyway, she will start screaming. 

__Marco__ Who? 

__Giorgio__ The old lady, the wife. 

__Marco__ No, she won’t. 

_They stare at each other in silence for a while. Then Marco’s phone starts ringing with the theme song of the movie “Rocky”_
