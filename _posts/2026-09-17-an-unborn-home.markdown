title:  An Unborn Home
author: Anindita Basu

excerpt: They say souls must find a seashore / before they can return home. // There is no sea / where my mother is from.

They say souls must find a seashore  
before they can return home.

There is no sea  
where my mother is from.

Will you send her a postcard?  
Watercolor waves,  
a small white boat  
afloat on the fading horizon,  
and silent seagulls  
against the silver sky?

Will you ask the wise man  
who lives on the mountain  
how many waves must dash against the cliffs  
before a postcard becomes a sea?

How many of them must lose their way  
before she, my mother,  
finds a way to the shore?

And when she finally arrives,  
salt-haired and grey,  
will you ask the wise man  
if she can know for sure---

she, who has never seen the sea---

that an unborn home  
is waiting to receive her  
in its cradle?
