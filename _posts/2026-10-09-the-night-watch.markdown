## The Night Watch

It was a bone-chilling winter night in the month of *Paush*. “Dad, I’m heading out then. Chiru, bring the cot.” The thing was, a lot of valuable belongings were lying outside the house that night, and there was no one at home to keep watch, so we took on the responsibility. I told Chiru, “Bring an iron spear along too. You never know what might happen on a dark night.” It was only 10 p.m. when Gautam came outside and said, “What are you doing out here in the middle of the dark night?” Chiru replied, “I’m waiting for a ghost. Want to join?” He said, “No, brother, spare me.” There was no way we were going to fall asleep that night anyway, so I thought, why not watch two or three movies?

I downloaded one and started watching it, leaning the iron spear against a pole. I wasn’t really interested in the movie, so I sat upright on the cot. As the night grew darker, I started hearing strange noises, and all the villagers’ ghost stories began running through my mind. I told Chiru, “No matter what happens today, we’re going to expose that ghost.” I checked the time—it was only 12 o’clock. Five more hours!! What was going to happen? “Jai Shri Ram!” Suddenly, a voice said, “Whatever happens, we’ll deal with it later. First look—the movie’s about to end.” But those strange sounds kept echoing in my ears. I asked, “Did you hear that sound just now?” No. Why? What happened? Nothing. Come on, let’s put on another movie. Then I interrupted myself and said, “Forget it. I’m not in the mood anymore.”

It was almost 1 a.m. Out of fear, I pulled the iron spear closer and asked him, “You’ve been here for years. What’s the story?”It’s nothing. Once, Gautam slept here too. That night, he heard the tinkling sound of anklets. What are you saying!? “I’m telling you the truth.” “Alright then. If something like that happens, there are two of us—we’ll handle it somehow.” Talking like this, it became 3 a.m. I said, “The next two hours are going to be very important.” Soon I heard snoring. Chiru had fallen asleep, but I couldn’t sleep at all. Anyway, to pass the time, I started playing a game. Suddenly, I heard a faint metallic rattling sound from somewhere. I got scared.

“Chiru, wake up! Brother, wake up!!” I gripped the spear tightly. “What’s the matter? Why are you shouting?” “Brother... a ghost... a ghost!” Just then, a voice called out: “Akash... Akash... Akash... Akash.” “Yes, Dad, I’m coming!” Dad had woken up. A slight smile appeared on my face, and to encourage myself, I shouted loudly, “Hey, get up! It’s 5 o’clock. Morning’s here.” Just then, the rattling sound suddenly became much louder...

...and an express train roared right in front of me!
