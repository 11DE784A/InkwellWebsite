# Submission Guidelines

These guidelines are not rules to restrict you, but a shared understanding to
help contributors feel confident and the magazine feel cohesive.

_Our core belief is simple: everyone has something worth saying. These
guidelines exist to support that, not to get in the way._


## Our Philosophy

The Inkwell is a free and open space. We do not have a house style or a
preferred subject matter. We believe in:

- Creative freedom: write what you want, how you want it.
- Diversity of voices: we actively welcome contributions from all backgrounds,
  cultures, and disciplines.
- No hierarchy of quality: a first-time writer is as welcome as a seasoned
  author.
- Kindness and respect towards fellow contributors, readers, and the community
  at large.


## What We Accept

Almost anything. Here are some examples of what we'd love to receive, but this
list is not exhaustive. If you're thinking of something not listed here, send
it anyway.

### Written contributions

- Poetry, any form, any length, any language.
- Short stories and flash fiction.
- Personal essays and reflections.
- Opinion pieces, manifestos, or commentary.
- Reviews: books, films, music, exhibitions, restaurants, anything.
- Letters to a person, a place, a memory, an idea.
- Reflections on home, culture, identity, language, or belonging.
- Observations on life as a researcher: the joys, the struggles, the absurdities.
- Travel writing, food writing, nature writing.
- Anything else you feel like writing.

### Visual contributions

- Illustrations and drawings.
- Photography.
- Graphic art and digital art.
- Comics or visual storytelling.
- Artwork from the SISSA Art Club is especially welcome.

## Tone & Style

There is no required tone or style. Write formally or informally. Be funny or
serious. Be personal or analytical. Use your own voice; that is what makes the
magazine interesting.

A few gentle suggestions:

- Write with sincerity. Readers can feel when something is genuine.
- You don't need to be an expert on your topic. Your perspective is enough.
- If writing in a language other than English, that is absolutely welcome; please include a short English summary or translation so more readers can engage.
- There is no minimum or maximum word count. Say what you need to say.

## Language

The Inkwell welcomes contributions in any language. English is our primary
language of publication, but we celebrate the linguistic diversity of the SISSA
community.

- Contributions in languages other than English are welcome and encouraged.
- We will never ask you to change the language of your piece.
- We do not correct grammar or spelling in ways that alter your voice; light proofreading only.

## What We Don't Publish

We are open to almost everything, including controversial, political, and
challenging content. The only things we will not publish are:

- Content that is hateful, discriminatory, or targets individuals or groups based on identity.
- Content that harasses, bullies, or demeans specific real people.
- Content that violates copyright (e.g. reproducing others' work without permission).
- Content that is factually presented as true but is deliberately misleading.
- Content that endorses explicitly or implicitly, any colonialist or imperialist views, fascist ideologies, or any form of human injustice or oppression.

_If a piece touches on sensitive topics, personal trauma, political conflict, or
difficult experiences, we handle it with care and will always discuss it with
the contributor before publishing._

## Editorial Process

Our editorial process is light-touch and collaborative:


- All submitted pieces are read by at least one member of the SISSA Magazine Club.
- We may offer light feedback or suggestions, but nothing is changed without the contributor's knowledge and agreement.
- Contributors will always be credited unless they request anonymity.
- We will confirm receipt of every submission and keep contributors updated on timelines.

## Rights & Ownership

- All contributors retain full ownership and copyright of their work.
- By submitting, contributors grant The SISSA Inkwell the right to publish
  their piece in print and digitally on our website and social media.
- We will never publish work elsewhere or commercially without explicit
  permission.

## How to Submit

You can send your contribution to us via email at {{ site.obf_email }} __with the
word "submission" somewhere in the subject line__. 

Please send your written contribution in one of the following ways: text pasted
in the email message, markdown formatted plain text, an DOCX/ODT file or Google
Doc. Please __do not__ use fancy formatting in your submission and __do not__
send PDFs.

Please send your artwork as PNG or JPEG at a sufficiently high resolution.

## A Final Word

These guidelines are a living document. As the magazine grows, we will revisit
and update them together as a community. If you have suggestions, questions, or
concerns about anything here, please reach out; we want this to be something we
build together.

Welcome to the Inkwell. We can't wait to read you. ✦
